Characters

Abrams

TANK

BRAWLER

BULL-HEADED

A hard-drinking New York detective investigating a mysterious tome left on his desk. He heals by draining nearby enemies, charges into groups and stuns them against walls. Great if you like starting fights up close and soaking up damage.

Apollo

FINESSE

MOBILITY

A CUT ABOVE

A young prince of Ixia and fencing captain, sent to New York for safety. He lunges, parries attacks to stun the attacker and slashes from range with his ultimate. Great if you enjoy precise timing, mobility and duelling single targets.

Baba

CACKLING

BEADY

HEXING

A pigeon lady who has apparently sat on one Central Park bench for fifty years, guarding the city from Patron-summoners. She drains enemy spirit, brews tea that shields or silences, and hexes foes into pigeons. Great for spooky, spell-heavy brawling.

Bebop

HOOK

BOMB

PUNCH

A polite scrap golem who fights in underground arenas to pay for his adoptive mother's medicine. He hooks targets, plants stacking bombs and finishes with a healing beam. Great if you like pulling enemies out of position.

Billy

PUNK

CHAOTIC

G.O.A.T.

A furious punk raging at "The Man", with a mostly tongue-in-cheek backstory. He rams and slams enemies, refills ammo with melee hits and yanks foes in with a chain. Great if you want constant close-range pressure.

Calico

TRICKSY

SLIPPERY

BURST DAMAGE

A former monster hunter who found that contract killing pays better. She throws delayed bombs, leaps in to slash and heal, then vanishes into shadow. Great if you like ambushing, stalking and slipping away with good timing.

Celeste

PERFORMER

DISRUPTIVE

DAZZLING

A Coney Island sideshow star who is secretly a unicorn hiding from poachers. She floats above fights, blasts enemies for spirit lifesteal and shields herself with a barrier that explodes. Great if you like chaining ability combos from the air.

Deadman Danny

RECKLESS

HIGH-FLYING

EXPLOSIVE

Fairfax Industries' star salesman, a skeleton who demos company weapons live on New York's streets. He flies a jetpack, fires spirit missiles and sprays fuel that shreds bullet resistance, then grabs an enemy and slams them down. Great for daring dive-bombers.

The Doorman

DISORIENTING

MAP CONTROL

MIND GAMES

A polite fledgling god who got bored of murder and stayed on as a hotel doorman to watch humanity. He throws a slowing bell, links two doors across the map and drags enemies with a luggage cart. Great if you like setups and map control.

Drifter

STALKER

BLOODTHIRSTY

CRUEL

An ancient vampire who ignores vampire law, so the Viscounts and Elders want him dead. He marks targets and teleports behind them, hitting isolated enemies harder. Great if you like picking off stragglers and flanking more than team brawls.

Dynamo

TEAMPLAY

INITIATOR

CLUTCH

A Columbia professor reduced to a dying star by a rift in Central Park, now wishing for a body. He launches enemies, teleports allies, heals with an aura and stuns groups with Singularity. Great for supporting your team and swinging fights.

Graves

MORBID

AREA DENIAL

NECROMANCER

A teenager who found a severed lich hand and now handles the dead's unfinished business. She summons ghouls and Deadheads, pins enemies with grasping hands and wears them down over time. Great if you like summons and zone control.

Grey Talon

PRECISION

HUNTER

AREA DENIAL

A founding Baxter Society monster hunter who left retirement after a suspicious fire killed his son's family. He charges piercing arrows, snares enemies and steers a stunning spirit owl. Great if you like sharpshooting and setting traps.

Haze

ASSASSIN

STEALTHY

LETHAL

A secret agent who enters targets' dreams to judge their threat, then kills the ones deemed too dangerous. She puts enemies to sleep, turns invisible and unloads Bullet Dance. Great for stealthy gunplay and picking off targets.

Holliday

CRACKSHOT

EXPLOSIVE

APPREHENDER

Granddaughter of the gunslinger Doc Holliday, a sheriff chasing a murderous musician to New York. She throws explosive barrels, bounces heroes skyward and lassos targets in. Great if you love headshots and mobility tricks.

Infernus

ARSONIST

EXPLOSIVE

BURN RUBBER

An Ixian bartender with fire powers and a volatile temper. He sprays napalm, dashes through flames and stacks burns, then detonates a blast that stuns everyone nearby. Great if you like aggressive close-range burn-down fights.

Ivy

TEAM-UP

DISRUPTOR

ROCK SOLID

A gargoyle brought to life to protect a family from a crime syndicate, now seeking a new purpose. She tethers to allies, slows foes with thorns and carries a teammate across the map. Great for enabling and rescuing teammates.

Kelvin

PROTECTOR

EXPLORER

ICE COLD

An undead scientist found frozen after an Arctic expedition, with no memory. He heals allies with frost grenades, lays an ice path for speed and shelters teammates in an ice dome. Great if you want a sturdy support who still shoots.

Lady Geist

LIFESTEAL

SELF DAMAGE

FATALE

A socialite who made a pact with a spirit to regain her youth, and now feeds on others' life force. Many abilities cost her health, but she drains enemies to heal. Great if you enjoy risky play and managing your own health.

Lash

INITIATOR

HIGH-FLYING

ARROGANT

A brash dockworker and underground fight-club champion with a huge ego. He grapples, dive-stomps and whips enemies, then lifts and slams a whole group with his ultimate. Great if you love mobility and diving into fights.

McGinnis

INVENTOR

SUPPORT

DISRUPTION

Maggie McGinnis heads a company's military research and treats the supernatural as something to build with. She deploys turrets, summons healing spirits, raises slowing walls and fires homing rockets. Great if you like fortifying positions with gadgets.

Mina

HARASSER

NIMBLE

VEXING

A wealthy heiress turned vampire who wants to become Viscount of New York. She slashes weakened targets with her umbrella, flies as untargetable bats and adds spirit damage to her shots. Great for slippery hit-and-run play.

Mirage

BODYGUARD

TRAVELLER

FOCUSED

A human bodyguard to a Djinn ambassador negotiating to buy part of Wyoming. He infests enemies with life-stealing scarabs, lifts them in a whirlwind and teleports across the map. Great if you like roaming and mixing gunplay with abilities.

Mo & Krill

TAG-TEAM

INITIATOR

BURROWER

Tunnel Rats who run illicit trade underground and punish gangs that abuse the tunnels. They burrow and erupt to knock enemies up, disarm with sand and lock a target in a stun. Great for ambushing as a tanky, annoying brawler.

Paige

HELPFUL

PROTECTOR

BOOKSMART

A bibliomancer searching for her younger brother, who vanished after dabbling in forbidden magic. She sends a burning dragon, gives allies barriers and marks areas that detonate to immobilize. Great for protecting teammates while shooting from range.

Paradox

CALCULATED

DISRUPTOR

TACTICIAN

Both a hero and a thieves' guild that enjoys embarrassing corporations and governments. She freezes enemy bullets, stops enemies in time with a charged shot and swaps places with an enemy hero. Great for clever setups and pick-off plays.

Pocket

TRICKSTER

BURST DAMAGE

FROGS

Arin Fairfax, heir to a major company, was shot and left for dead at 18 and now hides their identity. They teleport to a thrown cloak, hide in a suitcase and unleash a curse that blocks healing. Great for close-range bursts.

Rat King

SCRAPPY

REGAL

TENACIOUS

Louis, rightful King of the New York rats, plans a rat nation called Ratannia on Long Island. He slows enemies with scrap grenades, deflects bullets with a barrier and buffs allies with his banner. Great for a sturdy frontliner.

Rem

HELPFUL

TINY

ZZZZZ

A sleepy child from a dream world who ended up in New York by accident and just wants to get home. He tosses pillows, naps beside allies to heal them and puts enemies to sleep. Great for healing and crowd control.

Seven

HIGH VOLTAGE

MERCILESS

AREA DENIAL

A megalomaniac scientist who killed his captors when the wards meant to destroy his soul failed, and who now chases apotheosis. He throws lightning, marks enemies for stuns and calls down a storm cloud. Great for zoning with area damage.

Shiv

RAGE

BLEED

REPEAT

A former criminal atoning by hunting monsters for the Baxter Society, distrusted but effective. He throws bleeding knives, dashes through foes and executes weakened enemies. Great if you like flanking and finishing kills.

Silver

FERAL

HOT MESS

TRANSFORMATION

Lilah Silver, a New York bounty hunter and werewolf who insists she has it under control. She reloads instantly, kicks and pins targets, then transforms into a werewolf. Great for aggressive brawling with a big payoff.

Sinclair

TRICKSTER

COPYCAT

VERSATILE

Two stage magicians bound together who died in a stage accident and now haunt the world as bickering ghosts. They fire redirectable bolts, hex enemies into rabbits and copy enemy ultimates. Great for adapting on the fly.

Solomon

STRATEGIST

FOCUSED

CHECKMATE

A calm chess player who treats the whole city as a chessboard. His abilities are chess pieces: slowing bishops, shielding knights, immobilizing rooks and a crashing queen. Great if you like planning zones and combos.

Venator

DEVOUT

ARMS EXPERT

TACTICAL

Father Quinn Rourke, a hardened Vatican-sanctioned monster hunter sent to destroy New York's evil. He throws burning grenades, sets traps and fires three crossbow stakes, the last of which executes. Great for a weapon-damage hunter with crowd control.

Victor

YOU

CAN'T

KILL ME

Wakes on a mortuary table with no memory, searching for whoever his body parts came from. He charges up from damage taken, fires shocks and revives after a lethal hit. Great for risky, durable fighting.

Vindicta

SNIPER

SOARING

ONE SHOT KILL

A girl killed in the Salem Witch Trials who returned to protect supernatural beings. She flies, tethers enemies with a stake and fires a scoped shot that hits low-health targets hard. Great for precise aerial sniping.

Viscous

EVASIVE

DISRUPTOR

GOOEY

A psychic sea anemone from under the Bermuda Triangle, living in a goo body to protect his home. He bounces slowing goo, encases targets in a cube and rolls as a stunning ball. Great for slippery, playful disruption.

Vyper

GUNNER

SLIPPERY

RAT-A-TAT

An impulsive outlaw who keeps escaping every prison she lands in. She stacks slowing daggers, poisons wounded targets, slides far and petrifies with an explosive bola. Great for constant movement and a fast automatic gun.

Warden

INITIATOR

FEARLESS

ONE MAN ARMY

Raised by militant alchemists as a weapon to keep the Patrons out of our world. He throws weakening flasks, curses targets to immobilize them and heals with his ultimate's pulses. Great if you want to dive in and win scraps.

Wraith

DUELIST

ISOLATOR

TELEKINETIC

A New York gambling boss who stays out of the law's reach through bribery and blackmail. She throws suit-based cards, teleports in and out of fights and slams enemies with telekinesis. Great for focusing one target.

Yamato

RELENTLESS

ACROBATICS

PURSUER

She took her late brother's name after a coup killed him, to carry on his dream. She charges sword slashes, grapples to enemies and turns briefly invincible. Great for chaining close-range kills.
